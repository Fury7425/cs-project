## founding
- Night of 4 Dec 1884, post-office banquet: Queen Min's nephew 민영익 is cut seven times by swords. A 26-year-old American doctor who had never worked in a hospital pushes past 14 royal physicians and stitches him up 'all blood and gore'. Rumors spread that he revived a dead prince, and crowds gather at his gate.
- Min worries that the women of his house might 'poison' his wounds just by looking at them. Allen laughs it off, then the wound gets inflamed and the court doctors blame the women. This shows the clash between traditional and Western medicine.
- April 1885, 재동: the new hospital is in the confiscated house of 홍영식, a coup leader who had just been killed. Notices at the four city gates and Bosingak announce free treatment from 1 to 5 p.m. The first inpatient is a young man with a rotting thigh bone, operated on while an untrained assistant handles the anesthesia.
- Payment at 제중원 was 'no cure, no pay'. Grateful patients came back with hundreds of eggs, live pigs, chickens and pheasants instead of money. Good for a funny game scene where the doctor's yard fills with animals.
- Underwood almost became a pastor at home. His acceptance letter was ready to mail when he heard an inner voice say 'No one for Korea.' He walked into the Presbyterian board office once more and learned another candidate had just dropped out, so the post was his.
- Easter Sunday, 5 April 1885, 제물포: Underwood and the Appenzellers step ashore together. Christian preaching is still illegal, so Underwood starts as a chemistry and physics teacher at Allen's hospital.
- 11 May 1886, 정동: Underwood's orphanage school opens with exactly one boy. Up to then he had secretly baptized Mr. No (11 July 1886), a man who stole two gospels and read them all night.
- Tuesday evening, 27 Sept 1887: in a small house in 정동, 14 Korean believers and Underwood organize Korea's first Presbyterian church, 새문안교회.
- 1888 'Baby Riots': rumors say the foreigners at the hospital eat children, and mobs threaten them. Underwood spends the summer in a hut in the city, building a boys' school anyway.
- March 1889: Underwood marries Dr. Lillias Horton, Queen Min's physician. Their honeymoon is a 1,000-mile trek to the northern border, including a standoff with robbers. That autumn the Queen sends them gold bracelets and a pearl ring.
- July 1890: Dr. Heron, who gave up a Tennessee professorship for Korea, catches dysentery from his patients and dies at about 32, leaving his wife and two daughters. He becomes the first person buried at 양화진. His last words were 'Jesus loves you... Stand by Him!'
- 1893–94: the Canadian Dr. Avison finds 제중원 drained by corrupt officials. After six months of negotiation (late Sept 1894, at the time of the 갑오개혁 and the Sino-Japanese War), the government hands the hospital to the mission. During cholera he plasters Seoul with 50,000 posters saying germs, not spirits, cause the disease.
- 1900, Carnegie Hall, New York: after Avison's speech, businessman Louis Severance comes up to him and offers money for a new hospital. Severance Hospital opens in 1904 and leads directly to today's Yonsei medical school.
## pillars
- 1900년 4월 카네기홀: 에비슨이 '일곱 개의 부실한 병원보다 하나의 좋은 병원'을 호소하자, 낯선 신사 세브란스가 다가와 계획을 묻는다. 에비슨이 '그런 병원 계획서가 지금 제 주머니에 있습니다'라며 고든의 설계도를 꺼낸다.
- 1902년 11월 남대문 밖: 신제중원 머릿돌 놓는 날의 초청장. 1904년 9월 23일 복숭아골에 세브란스병원이 문을 열고, 오후 5시 열쇠를 여는 의식이 열린다.
- 백정 박성춘은 죽을 병에서 에비슨에게 치료받은 뒤 백정 해방 탄원서를 쓴다. 그 아들 봉출(박서양)은 1908년 한국 최초의 면허 의사 7명 중 하나가 된다. 백정의 아들이 의사 가운을 입는 장면.
- 1908년 6월 3일 오후 4시: 테니스장 위 대형 텐트와 의자 700석, 하객 약 1,000명. 이토 히로부미가 귀빈석에 앉은 가운데 조선인 졸업생 7명이 졸업장을 받는 긴장된 순간.
- 김필순의 집과 안창호, 신민회. 105인 사건 체포망이 좁혀 오자 1911년 말 김필순은 동료 의사에서 망명객이 되어 만주로 떠나고, 1919년 치치하얼에서 의문의 죽음을 맞는다.
- 1915년 4월 12일 종로 YMCA 빌린 교실: 원두우가 조선기독교대학을 연다. 1년 반 뒤인 1916년 10월 12일, 그는 신촌 캠퍼스를 보지 못한 채 애틀랜틱시티에서 숨진다.
- 1917년 형 존 언더우드의 기부금으로 연희궁 옛터 근처 창천리 약 19만 평을 산다. 1919년 4월 3·1운동 한복판에서 스팀슨관 머릿돌이 놓인다.
- 1919년 2월 28일: 제자 이갑성이 지팡이 짚은 스코필드 교수에게 선언서를 건네며 '사진을 찍어 세계에 알려 달라'고 부탁한다.
- 1919년 3월 5일 남대문역 광장: 연희전문생 김원벽이 인력거 위에서 '조선독립' 깃발을 흔들고 수만 군중이 환호한다. 그는 체포되어 징역 2년을 받는다.
- 1919년 4월 제암리: 스코필드가 경찰을 피해 불탄 교회 터에 들어가 셔터를 누른다. 그는 '34번째 민족대표'가 되고, 1920년 쫓겨나듯 한국을 떠난다.
## darkage
- 1941, 핀슨관 attic: 윤동주 hand-copies 19 poems three times; 이양하 tells him 'too nationalistic, wait.' The one copy that survives is the one he gave away.
- 광양 망덕포구, 1944-ish: 정병욱 is drafted; tells his mother 'keep this until I come back alive.' She wraps it in silk, puts it in a jar under the 마루 — the only copy that survives.
- 1945-02-16 03:36, Fukuoka prison: 윤동주 dies at 27; cousin 송몽규 dies in the same prison 1945-03-07. Six months before liberation.
- 1942-08-17: the Underwood statue is torn down and a stone 興亞紀念塔 rises in its place; 연희 becomes 'enemy property' under a Japanese principal; 1944 even the name disappears (경성공업경영전문학교).
- 1942-10-01: 조선어학회 members arrested, hauled to 홍원; 최현배 gets 4 years in 함흥, 이윤재 dies in a cold cell 1943-12-08. 최현배 walks out only after liberation (1945-08-17).
- 1938: 백남운 and fellow economics professors plus students arrested from 연희 — his prison mugshot at 44 survives.
- 1941-12: 원한경, born in Seoul, interned the day Pearl Harbor hits; deported May 1942; returns after liberation — and 1949-03-17 his wife Ethel is shot in their own house during a faculty wives' gathering.
- 1957-01-05: 연희 + 세브란스 = 연세 — the name itself is the story of two schools that survived the dark age.
